1. **Flying to the Cruise-Port in Miami: Predicting Passenger Satisfaction in Cruise-Linked Air Travel Using Machine Learning**
   - DOI：10.2139/ssrn.6475299 ｜ 2026 ｜  ｜ 被引 0 ｜ posted-content
   - 摘要（原文）：This study investigates the operational drivers of airline passenger satisfaction within the specific setting of cruise-linked air travel to the Port of Miami. Using an anonymized survey dataset that combines service-quality ratings, passenger attributes, and a verified satisfaction label, we benchmark seven supervised classifiers, including Logistic Regression, Linear SVM, Decision Tree, Random Forest, XGBoost, LightGBM, and CatBoost. To test how information is represented and compressed, we evaluate three feature designs: (i) fourteen service-rating factors, (ii) a single engineered Total Satisfaction score formed as the sum of the fourteen factors, and (iii) the combined representation (fourteen factors plus the aggregate score). Models are evaluated using accuracy, balanced accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, ROC-AUC, ROC curves, and confusion matrices. Across models, the representation based on the fourteen service factors yields strong discrimination, with boosting/ensemble methods achieving near-ceiling ROC-AUC (approximately 0.99) and accuracy around 0.95. In contrast, compressing the experience into a single Total Satisfaction feature produces substantially weaker discrimination (ROC-AUC $\approx 0.79$) and causes most models to converge to nearly identical ROC behavior, indicating that representational compression, rather than model choice, becomes the bottleneck. Finally, using Shapley Additive Explanations (SHAP), we show that top three features importance are Inflight wifi service, Online boarding, and Departure/Arrival time convenient. This top-three feature importance is consistent under passengers' age, gender, travel class, type of travel, and customer type. This implies the robust evidence that inflight connectivity and digital touchpoints are core drivers of satisfaction for cruise-bound travelers.
